# v2.9 — The Eventide Chain

## Explore beyond Vesper Gate

The Sol Gateway still leads to Coldharbor and the original Farside cluster. From Vesper Gate, a new connected branch reaches:

- **Lantern Twins:** adjacent red and blue stars; Twins Exchange provisions expeditions.
- **Aureole:** an inhabited research ring overlooks a black hole and luminous accretion disk.
- **Reliquary:** a pulsar illuminates a graveyard of abandoned hulls. A marked radiation pocket warns before shield-draining pulses; docking lanes remain safe.
- **Nightjar:** a starless, geothermal rogue-world refuge and emergency beacon support rescue crews.
- **Glasswake:** a shattered world, working foundry, and rich ore/shard fields.
- **Palinode:** a research annex beside an ancient builders' observatory; discoveries deepen the mystery without opening another Gateway.

The branch loops through Aureole–Reliquary–Nightjar and continues through Glasswake to Palinode. Its boards offer authored research supply and survey-crew rescue contracts alongside repeatable survey → derelict → data-core expeditions. The original ten-system expedition route remains unchanged when offered there.

## Visuals and continuity

Binaries are two adjacent stationary background stars. Stations use stable docking coordinates with a matching landing-view perspective. Black holes do not exert gameplay gravity and no physical orbital simulation is introduced. Meridian, Gloam and Slagforge receive selected new sky treatments; their existing worlds, links and identities are retained.

Station landing artwork now depicts its actual in-space design, including Luna. Earth is renamed **Terra** in player-facing text; existing saves and stable object references remain intact.

## Smaller fixes

- Delivery contracts require another destination, never the planet/port offering them. Flying to another destination within the same system is valid.
- Solo pause no longer consumes incoming-hail deadlines. Expired or unavailable NPC transmissions remain readable in a bounded history; they cannot perform stale actions. Leaving the system still clears visit-local conversations.
- Combat Assist and the v2.8.3 catalog-cache fixes are unchanged.

## Playing the release

Download the complete **Quantum-Rift-v2.9.0.zip** release attachment, not a source ZIP containing Git LFS pointers. Close any older game server CMD window before launching the new copy, then confirm version **2.9.0**. Back up your Solo save before upgrading. Existing saves require no manual migration.
