# Quantum Rift 0.3.4.3

Public release: **0.3.4.3**. The source package and release tag retain **3.4.3** and **v3.4.3**, respectively.

## Blue cockpit

The blue Pressure Hull cockpit replaces the previous interface for flight,
station services, the galaxy map and chat. Open the ordinary game address;
no special URL or setting is needed. Spaceport news stays beside the world
illustration, or below the service panel on narrow screens. Hail is available
while landed as well as in flight. Saved Tray widths, enlarged menu tabs,
compact chat history and long dialog choices remain usable across screen sizes.

## Starting out

- Building Station Tier 3 now confirms the change and carries newcomer protection
  over until another 5 kills, 30 jumps or 150 ship power, whichever comes first.
  Captains already past Tier 3 do not regain protection.
- Newly spawned ambient raids and war fleets scale to the strongest captain flying
  in the system, with the weakest available hull as a minimum. Existing traffic,
  empty systems and Farside are not reduced by this scaling.
- Combat job danger compares enemy strength with your ship power. Assassination
  ratings include bodyguards; patrol ratings are estimates, not a guarantee about
  every ship already present or other captains arriving.
- The Clear a raider career goal brings a lone practice opponent on arrival in
  ordinary space. The Caravel Trader now includes a Cinder Driver in its stock gun mount.

## Other improvements

Research and crew bonuses have clearer descriptions, and the Personal Station
refinery is named Workshop. L lands at a selected world or mission destination.
Wide, short galaxy maps keep readable system details beside the chart. Busy
combat reuses projectile graphics instead of rebuilding them each frame.
Two supplied Wave Function Collapse recordings are included.

The beta Solo save generator catalog matches version 3.4.3 and retains its
captain, credits, ship and station controls. Gameplay screenshots show the standard
blue cockpit.

## Updating

Close the old server before starting the new copy. Back up and preserve existing
Solo and friend-game save folders. Download **Quantum-Rift-latest.zip**, extract
it, and follow the [README](../README.md) and its runtime instructions. The complete
archive includes compiled modules and bundled libraries; players do not need to
install npm dependencies. The game remains in beta.
