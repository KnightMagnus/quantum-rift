# Quantum Rift 0.5.2

## Flight and fleets

- Ready wingmates share cargo space and passenger seats with the lead ship.
- Followers can catch up after delayed jumps.
- Combat assist conserves guided ammunition and recovers stalled approaches.
- The cockpit shows ammunition guidance, rescue timing and encounter health.

## Missions and cockpit

- Captains can invite another player to help with eligible story encounters. Both players must consent.
- The Captain window includes objective guidance and an encounter log.
- Mission progression and flight feedback fixes reduce stalled objectives and unclear next steps.

## Worlds and stations

- Systems have distinct visual identities, with updated planets, moons, scenery and local flight scenes.
- Stations have refreshed hulls, tier variants and landing views.
- Farside Foothold adds another port to visit.

This remains a beta release. Back up your complete save directory while the game is stopped before updating.
