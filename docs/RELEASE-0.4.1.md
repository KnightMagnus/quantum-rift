# Quantum Rift 0.4.1

## Changes

- Find mining weapons and equipment under the outfitter’s MINING filter. Ship Details previews equipped outfits, including factory weapons, with sale prices. Sell equipment there while landed at an outfitter.
- Use I for Ship Details and O for Captain. Settings remains available through its button; both information shortcuts can be rebound.
- Open a compact loan panel with BANK beside your landed credits instead of a full Bank tab. Banking locations and loan rules are unchanged.
- Keep landed service tabs and Depart visible on short displays, with one main content scroller. Crew, Outfitter, Community and Exchange layouts adapt to narrow screens.
- Keep the Tray reachable while landed, and keep friend, message and Armada drafts when the panel refreshes.
- View colony artwork beside management on wide screens and as a smaller image on short screens. Colony actions and production rules are unchanged.
- Keep the space behind the sculpted HUD frame corners visible; rectangular outer shadows no longer fill the transparent corners.
- Clarify that both captains must be at their own Personal Station for player trading. Guest docking remains available.

## Updating

Close the old game server, back up your complete Solo or friend-game save folder, and extract the new download into a separate folder. Preserve your saves when updating. See the [README](../README.md) and [hosting guide](HOSTING.md).

The optional save generator remains Solo-only. This update does not change the network protocol or save schema.
