# Quantum Rift 0.3.5.1

Public release: **0.3.5.1**. The source package and release tag retain **3.5.1** and **v3.5.1**, respectively.

## Combat and jobs

- Ambient raiders share a limit based on your fitted weapons, defenses and active wingmates. Arrival groups, reinforcements and incursions use the same limit rather than adding independent waves.
- Combat ratings and job danger estimates use fitted firepower and durability. Weapons with missing beam reach or short projectile life have corrected ranges.
- Ordinary bounty offers match your fleet more closely and pay more for larger targets. An NPC finishing your marked target no longer leaves the contract stuck; rewards still require your participation.
- Relief passenger jobs identify a pickup port before the destination. Passengers must board there before delivery counts.
- Expired job offers refresh the board with an explanation instead of silently doing nothing.
- The default wingmate loot-order key is **apostrophe (')**, separate from the hail inbox key. Settings shows the current binding.
- Personal Station upgrade logs identify your station rather than Quantum Station.

## Concordance

The Constitution-class hull is now named **Concordance**. Its hull ID is unchanged, so existing ships and loadouts remain compatible. Captains' custom ship names are preserved.

## Updating

Close the old server before starting the new copy. Back up and preserve existing Solo and friend-game save folders. Download **Quantum-Rift-latest.zip**, extract it, and follow the [README](../README.md) and its runtime instructions. The game remains in beta. The beta Solo save generator catalog matches source version 3.5.1 and remains Solo-only.
