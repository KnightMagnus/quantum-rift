# Quantum Rift v2.9.1 — Independent captains

## Newcomer truce across the main galaxy

Before your Personal Station reaches **Station Tier 3 (T-3)**, ambient factions and pirates overlook you throughout the main galaxy, including high-danger and Umbral systems. Farside keeps its existing hostility rules.

This is **targeting protection, not invulnerability**. Crossing existing hostile beams/projectiles can still damage your ship. Six-second arrival grace remains separate. Avoid busy battle lines.

Bounty targets and other accepted combat encounters still fight their participants. Deliberate manual attacks, explicit wingmate Attack orders and selected-target Combat Assist remain available. Actual damage permits normal faction retaliation for the current system visit and normal standing penalties. Passive wingmates and passive Assist do not start an unwanted ambient fight merely because you are passing through or take incidental fire. PvP, agreed duels and the Crucible are unchanged.

## Wingmate repairs at outfitters

Landed planets and stations with outfitters now offer **WINGMATE REPAIRS**: disabled stored wingmates show their free-repair countdown and quoted rush-repair button. The existing ten-minute free timer, fees and DOCKS repair option are unchanged. Repairs require an owned disabled ship, the outfitter service and sufficient credits; duplicate requests cannot charge twice.

## Clearer station progression names

Station progression now says **Station Tier**, with compact **T-#** indicators. Existing saves retain their internal `ops` field and all thresholds, prices and unlocks remain unchanged. The separate OPS companion tab still means operations, not station progression.

## Download

Use the complete `Quantum-Rift-v2.9.1.zip` release asset. Close the older server CMD/terminal window before starting the new copy; existing saves remain compatible.
