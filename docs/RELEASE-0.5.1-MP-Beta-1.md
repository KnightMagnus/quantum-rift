# Quantum Rift 0.5.1 MP Beta 1 - Balance Patch

## Balance and mission fixes

- Reduce the extra shields and armor in an early story battle so a prepared mid-tier ship or modest wing has a better chance. Weapons and escorts are unchanged.
- Give clearer preparation advice, objectives and reward descriptions.
- Count allied finishing blows toward the appropriate story encounter and recover interrupted encounters.
- Fix mission progression while docked. Eligible survey and salvage objectives can also advance in flight without an unrelated landing; physical deliveries still require their destination.

## Other changes

- Add Concord security-fleet hull artwork and coordinated patrols.
- Adjust cinematic pacing and background effects.

## Updating

Close the old game server, back up your complete Solo or friend-game save folder, and extract the new download into a separate folder. Follow the [README](../README.md) for launch instructions.

The optional save generator remains Solo-only. Combat tuning was checked with deterministic test encounters; it is not a measured player win rate. Physical touch devices, gamepads and screen-reader compatibility have not been verified for this release.
